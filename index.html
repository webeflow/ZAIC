About ZAIC

Zahid Ashraf's Institute for CSS — Pakistan's dedicated academy for competitive examination preparation

Our Mission & Vision

ZAIC (Zahid Ashraf's Institute for CSS) was established with a singular mission — to provide the highest quality CSS and PMS preparation in Pakistan. Unlike generic academies that dilute their focus, ZAIC is 100% dedicated to competitive examinations.

Our founder, Prof. Zahid Ashraf, is not just a teacher but a prolific author who has written multiple books on CSS subjects. These books are used by aspirants across Pakistan and are considered essential reading material for competitive exam preparation.

At ZAIC, we believe in a holistic approach — from subject mastery to personality development, from answer writing skills to interview preparation. Every aspect of the CSS journey is covered under one roof.
